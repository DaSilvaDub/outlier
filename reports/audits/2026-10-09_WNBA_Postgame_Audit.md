## 8. 4 Concrete Pipeline Upgrades to Improve Future Performance

### Upgrade 1: Team Assist Ceiling & Teammate Cannibalization Guard
- **Problem:** Stacking Chelsea Gray Over 5.5 AST and Jackie Young Over 6.5 AST required at least 13 assists between two players. In a half-court playoff game where a team scores 71 regulation points on 26 made field goals, 13 assists represents 50%+ of all team baskets.
- **Rule:** **Never recommend or parlay two player assist OVERs from the same team in the same game.** In `outlier_scrapers/alt_player_props.py` and parlay builders, enforce a mutual exclusion rule on teammate assist props.

### Upgrade 2: Automated Pre-Game Lineup Hook Trigger
- **Problem:** The analysis noted "Check Loyd before lock." Loyd played, but the ticket recommendation remained active without an automatic downgrade.
- **Rule:** When an analysis has a conditional dependency (`own_star_returning` or `own_star_out`), the pipeline must produce an explicit **IF/THEN hard kill branch**:
  - *If Jewell Loyd ACTIVE:* **KILL Chelsea Gray AST Over** immediately.

### Upgrade 3: Rebound Defense Matchup Split Gating
- **Problem:** Reese's rebound line at 9.5 was priced at `-175` despite a cold 20% L5 hit rate, driven entirely by season average (12.1).
- **Rule:** The pipeline must penalize rebound lines when facing a top-3 opponent rebound defense (e.g. NYL with Jonquel Jones), preventing the model or user from paying heavy juice on season averages that are compressed by matchup.

### Upgrade 4: Enforce Floor Scoring Ladders Over Low-Count Playmaking
- **Problem:** Rebounds and assists carry discrete step variance (hooks on 5 and 9). Scoring ladders (Reese 12.5 PTS, Canada 13.5 PTS) carry much higher consistency because free throws and continuous possession ensure higher event opportunities.
- **Rule:** Prioritize alternate floor points ladders (e.g. `-250` to `-350`) over `-175` rebound/assist hooks for parlay anchors.
